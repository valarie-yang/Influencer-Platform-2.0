# Product brief

## Users

- Individual creators and personal-brand operators.
- Content strategists and small production teams.
- Operators who need a repeatable bridge from content strategy to publishing execution.

## Job to be done

Given a creator persona and a monthly objective, help an operator produce an editable content plan, turn selected topics into scripts and production tasks, and track the work through a publishing board.

## Scope

The prototype covers persona input, AI-assisted analysis, topic planning, script drafting, production planning, and publishing operations. The browser is the current persistence boundary.

## Product decisions

- The unit of value is a completed workflow, not a single generated paragraph.
- Each step produces structured information consumed by the next step.
- User editing and confirmation remain visible.
- Deterministic Mock AI makes product behavior repeatable before connecting a live provider.

## Non-goals

This repository does not claim live social APIs, autonomous publishing, production model evaluation, creator revenue impact, or a production multi-tenant backend.
